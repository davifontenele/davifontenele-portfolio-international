# 01 — AuAg

## Making precious metals accessible through design.

**Fullscreen subtitle**

From business vision to product experience: UX/UI leadership, creative direction, and digital storytelling for an early-stage fintech.

**CTA:** Explore the case

### Overview

Turning a business concept into a tangible digital experience through product design, user experience leadership, and audiovisual storytelling.

Working directly with the CEO, I helped shape the digital experience of an early-stage fintech, connecting the company's commercial narrative with the design of an interactive product demonstration.

### Context

AuAg is a fintech startup developing a platform designed to make precious metals such as gold, silver, and platinum more accessible to individual investors.

Its proposed ecosystem combines precious metals investing, a payment card, and a rewards system linked to metals.

When I joined as Head of UX/UI, the company was refining its website while partnerships, operational processes, and financial infrastructure were still being structured ahead of investment.

The digital experience had two complementary purposes: introduce the concept to potential customers and communicate the business vision to prospective investors.

### The Challenge

How do you make a sophisticated financial product feel approachable, intuitive, and engaging before the business is fully operational?

The website needed to communicate credibility, explain a new value proposition, and generate interest among different audiences.

At the same time, the startup needed a tangible demonstration of its future product: an application that could communicate how users would navigate, invest, monitor their holdings, and interact with the proposed ecosystem.

The experience had to feel accessible and rewarding without sacrificing the clarity and seriousness expected of a financial product.

### Strategy & Design Decisions

**Connecting business vision and user experience**

Working in direct collaboration with the CEO, I participated in the ongoing definition and validation of the digital experience.

While the company's broader business objectives were established by its leadership, I independently proposed several UX and interaction design solutions intended to make the product easier to understand and more enjoyable to use.

**Reducing perceived complexity**

One of the guiding principles was to make precious metals investing feel less intimidating to consumers unfamiliar with these assets.

I explored intuitive interactions, immediate visual feedback, clear information hierarchy, and refined microinteractions to create a sense of simplicity and responsiveness.

**Designing the product journey**

Using Figma, I designed the application's user journeys, information architecture, interfaces, and interaction patterns.

These designs informed the development of a functional demonstration MVP covering the core experiences planned for the product.

**Selling the vision through audiovisual storytelling**

For the website, I combined AI-generated visual material with extensive editing, compositing, and motion graphics in Adobe After Effects.

The goal was to create a compelling audiovisual narrative that communicated the company's ambition, positioned the product as premium, and made the concept more tangible.

### The Solution

**A website designed for two audiences**

An engaging digital presentation combining product messaging, visual storytelling, and cinematic motion to address both potential users and investors.

**An interactive product demonstration**

A functional MVP based on the Figma experience, allowing visitors to explore the application's proposed features using a fictional starting balance.

The demonstration includes simulated product interactions but does not support real financial transactions or full user account management.

Together, these experiences allow the AuAg concept to be communicated through both a commercial narrative and an interactive demonstration.

### My Role

**Head of UX/UI**

Responsible for directing the product's user experience, designing application journeys and interfaces, proposing interaction patterns, shaping the website's visual narrative, and collaborating directly with the CEO throughout the design and validation process.

My work also included generative AI-assisted audiovisual production, video editing, motion graphics, and collaboration around the MVP implementation.

### Outcomes

- A website supporting the startup's presentation to potential customers and investors.
- A cohesive product experience designed in Figma.
- An interactive MVP demonstrating core user journeys with simulated funds.
- A stronger visual and functional representation of a business concept still in development.

As AuAg remains in its pre-investment stage, these outcomes represent design and product demonstration achievements rather than commercial adoption, revenue, or live financial activity.

### Expertise

Product Design Leadership · UX/UI Strategy · Interaction Design · User Journey Mapping · Information Architecture · Figma · Prototyping · MVP · Executive Collaboration · Creative Direction · Generative AI · Adobe After Effects · Motion Graphics · Digital Storytelling

# 03 — Reserva

## Turning a crisis into a brand-defining idea.

**Fullscreen subtitle**

How a viral campaign drove a 9,900% increase in e-commerce sales and helped shape a lasting brand philosophy.

**CTA:** Explore the case

### Overview

A creative response to a store robbery became an internationally recognized campaign, activated an emerging e-commerce operation, and helped establish a philosophy that would remain part of Reserva's brand identity for years.

### Context

Reserva, a Brazilian menswear brand, experienced a robbery at one of its stores.

Security camera footage captured the incident, providing the raw material for an unexpected creative opportunity.

Instead of communicating the event only as a crisis, the idea emerged to transform an adverse situation into a positive, humorous brand narrative.

### The Challenge

Create a fast, relevant, and sensitive response to a real incident without trivializing violence or adopting a conventional victim narrative.

The campaign also needed to contribute to the activation of Reserva's still-emerging e-commerce business.

### The Creative Idea

**When life gives you lemons, make lemonade.**

The security footage was reinterpreted and edited into a video that transformed an unexpected problem into a statement of attitude.

The concept relied on timing, storytelling, humor, and the ability to find a compelling message within real events.

Rather than relying on an elaborate advertising production, the campaign derived its power from the creative reinterpretation of existing footage.

### Execution & Activation

The video served as both a communication campaign and an e-commerce activation.

It generated approximately 300,000 views during its first weekend and received organic coverage from national and international media outlets.

During the campaign period, the brand's online sales reportedly increased from approximately US$500 to US$50,000 — a rise of roughly **9,900%**.

### Beyond the Campaign

The idea of turning lemons into lemonade went beyond the original video.

It became part of Reserva's internal language and public brand identity, returning in later campaigns, promotional initiatives, visual themes, and documentary-style content featuring entrepreneurs discussing the challenges of building businesses.

A short-term creative response helped establish a long-term brand narrative.

### My Role

Creative participation in the conception and collaborative execution of the campaign, including narrative development and the editing of security footage into a coherent audiovisual piece.

### Impact & Legacy

- Approximately 300,000 video views during the first weekend.
- Organic national and international media attention.
- Approximately 9,900% growth in e-commerce sales during the campaign period.
- A creative concept that remained associated with the brand for years.

**Seven years later, Reserva was sold in a transaction valued at R$715 million.**

That subsequent transaction represents a milestone in the company's broader trajectory, not a financial outcome attributable to this individual campaign.

### Expertise

Creative Strategy · Brand Storytelling · Crisis Communication · Creative Concept · Video Editing · Brand Activation · E-commerce Activation · Earned Media · Brand Positioning · Business Impact

# 04 — Espaço Fashion

## Turning fashion consumers into content creators.

**Fullscreen subtitle**

An iF Communication Design Award-winning experience combining fashion films, interactive design, and user-generated content.

**CTA:** Explore the case

### Overview

An award-winning interactive fashion experience that invited consumers to create and share their own digital catalogs using frames selected from the brand's campaign films.

### Context

For its Spring/Summer 2010–11 collection, Brazilian fashion brand Espaço Fashion collaborated with digital studio 6D on a campaign combining fashion films and an interactive online experience.

The creative direction established the campaign's overall visual language, which then needed to be translated into a digital environment that encouraged audience participation.

### The Challenge

Go beyond a conventional fashion catalog by giving consumers an active role in creating and sharing brand content.

The experience needed to connect audiovisual material, interface design, and interactive behavior in a cohesive and intuitive way.

### The Interactive Concept

Working from the campaign's established art direction, I conceived the interactive experience, designed its interfaces, and programmed its interactions.

Visitors could explore the fashion films, select individual frames, and use them to create personalized catalogs.

Their compositions could be shared on Facebook and published on the brand's website.

The project turned the audience from passive viewers into active participants in the brand's communication.

### My Role

Responsible for conceiving the digital interaction concept, designing the interfaces, and programming the interactive experience.

The work was developed within 6D's team under the campaign's established creative direction.

### Recognition & Impact

The project received an **iF Communication Design Award**, one of the internationally recognized distinctions in design.

Its participatory approach anticipated practices that would later become widely associated with user-generated content in digital marketing.

### Expertise

Interactive Experience Design · Creative Technology · UX/UI Design · Interaction Design · Creative Coding · Front-end Development · Digital Art Direction · User-Generated Content · Fashion Communication · Award-Winning Design

An interactive fashion website featuring original animations, internationally recognized typography, and four years of brand relevance.

**CTA:** Explore the case

### Overview

A highly interactive fashion website for Salinas, featuring interfaces, interactions, and animations created by me.

### The Challenge

Turn an existing visual direction into a distinctive digital environment where animation, typography, and interaction became integral to the brand experience.

# 06 — Rocinha UP

## Designing identity for social transformation.

**Fullscreen subtitle**

A dual brand identity connecting education, circular economy, and sustainable fashion in Rio de Janeiro's Rocinha community.

**CTA:** Explore the case

### Overview

A unified visual identity connecting professional education and sustainable fashion through two complementary brand expressions.

The project brings together Brazilian jiu-jitsu, upcycling, and income-generation opportunities in the Rocinha community.

### Context

Rocinha UP is a social initiative conceived by Paula Maia, founder of Faz Girar Market, in partnership with Paulo Cunha, founder of Projeto Lapidar Jiu-Jitsu.

The initiative connects professional training, circular economy principles, and sustainable product development through the reuse of discarded jiu-jitsu materials.

The challenge for design was to create a recognizable identity for an initiative with two interconnected dimensions: education and a sustainable fashion label.

### The Challenge

How can one brand communicate two distinct activities without losing coherence?

The identity needed to represent professional development, community belonging, environmental awareness, and contemporary design while maintaining a clear connection to the origins of the project.

### Brand Strategy & Concept

**One identity. Two expressions.**

Starting from an initial idea proposed by Paula Maia — using a jiu-jitsu belt as visual inspiration — I developed a symbol combining several layers of meaning.

Its geometry evokes a martial arts belt, while its overall silhouette also resembles a jewel, referencing Projeto Lapidar Jiu-Jitsu.

The idea of polishing a precious stone becomes a metaphor for developing human potential and transforming discarded materials into valuable products.

I proposed a dual visual system:

**The untied belt** represents education, training, and professional development.

**The tied belt** identifies the fashion label and its upcycled products.

Both versions belong to the same visual identity, allowing each activity to communicate its purpose while remaining part of a coherent brand.

### The Solution

I developed the brand identity system, including symbol, visual language, variations, iconography, digital applications, website, and brand guidelines.

The system was extended to communication materials, audiovisual content, product presentations, and institutional applications.

### My Role

Responsible for the visual identity concept, narrative brand system, art direction, and digital applications.

I worked with full creative autonomy from the initial visual reference provided by Paula Maia, who reviewed and approved the project deliverables.

The underlying social initiative and business concept had already been established by its founders.

### Recognition & Impact

Rocinha UP received support through FAPERJ's PISTA program and launched publicly in August 2026.

The initiative has begun its training activities, introduced initial upcycled products, and received media coverage, including television reporting and recognition by FAPERJ.

The identity now supports the public communication of an active initiative connecting education, sustainability, and creative economic opportunities.

### Expertise

Brand Strategy · Brand Identity · Brand Architecture · Visual Storytelling · Symbol Design · Art Direction · Design Systems · Digital Experience · Sustainability Communication · Social Impact · Iconography · Brand Guidelines

## My Leadership Philosophy

### Direction without micromanagement.

Great creative leadership isn't about having every answer. It's about asking the right questions, establishing a clear direction, and creating the conditions for people to do their best work.

I believe designers need space to explore, supported by thoughtful guidance, constructive feedback, and a consistent standard of quality.

I value open collaboration and clear communication between people with different skills and perspectives.

### Evidence before assumptions.

Understanding the problem comes before designing the solution.

My approach to discovery combines stakeholder conversations, qualitative interviews, quantitative research, benchmarking, and practical experience.

Research is an instrument for questioning assumptions, reducing uncertainty, and making more informed decisions — not simply a formality to validate an initial brief.

### Quality through collaboration.

Great design should maintain its integrity from concept to implementation.

My technical background helps me understand the challenges of development and communicate effectively with designers, engineers, and QA professionals.

I believe quality is a shared responsibility, supported by careful review, constructive collaboration, and attention to detail.
